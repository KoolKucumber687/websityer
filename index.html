06 / SAFETY FRAMEWORK

The Core Laws of ScamShield.

Autonomous defense for seniors requires constitutional safety parameters. Our multi-agent system operates under four foundational laws.

01

False Alarms Matter

A false positive destroys senior trust. If an elderly user gets panicked by a legitimate hospital or bank text, they abandon the tool. We calibrate our detection threshold for absolute zero false alarms.

Precision > Overreaction
02

Check the Claim, Not Just Pixels

Scammers can copy high-resolution logos, CSS, and familiar voices with ease. ScamShield examines systemic veracity: domain registration age, DNS authorities, out-of-band registries, and cryptographic roots.

Forensics > Surface Visuals
03

Official Verification First

Never follow caller-provided callbacks or verification links. ScamShield cross-references independent, officially vetted registries (FDIC records, verified postal endpoints, and municipal jail directories).

Out-of-Band Validation
04

When Uncertain, Output: "Be Careful"

Progressive friction over alarmism. If an agent has partial signal, it does not scream threat. It outputs a calm, guided advisory: "Be Careful", locks high-risk actions, and routes to a trusted family contact.

Graceful Fail-Safe
